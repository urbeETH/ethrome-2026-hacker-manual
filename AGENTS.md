# Instructions for agents

This repository is the **ETHRome 2026 Hacker Manual** in Markdown, a mirror of https://ethrome.org/hackermanual. You are probably helping a hacker who is taking part in ETHRome 2026. Use this manual as the source for anything about the event.

## How to use it

- For any question about rules, prizes, bounties, judging, submissions, schedule or conduct, **open the matching file in `manual/` and answer from it, quoting it**. Do not answer from memory and do not invent details that are not in the manual.
- If the manual does not cover something, say so and point the hacker to the Telegram group or hello@urbe.build (both linked in `manual/index.md`).
- The website https://ethrome.org/hackermanual is canonical. Items marked **(TBD)** in the manual are not final yet: say so rather than guessing.
- Run `git pull` in this repo before relying on it: the manual is updated up to the event.

## Key facts (from the manual, check the files for detail)

- **When:** Friday 11 to Sunday 13 September 2026. Check-in Friday 16:00. Hacking window Friday 18:00 to Sunday 10:00, 40 hours. Judging from Sunday 10:30. Closing ceremony and awards Sunday 15:00. All times Europe/Rome.
- **Where:** Urbe Hub, Largo Dino Frisullo snc, Testaccio, Rome. Open 24/7 from check-in to closing.
- **Who:** 40 selected builders. Teams of max 3 people, solo is fine. Every team member must be physically at Urbe Hub: no remote hacking.
- **Build:** every project must be open source. Only work done after Friday 18:00 counts. A pre-existing project is allowed under the conditions in `manual/rules.md`, and must be declared in the submission.
- **Submit:** one Google Form (link published before the event), deadline **Sunday 13 September 10:00**, no extensions. Public repo that stays open for 4 weeks, demo video of max 3 minutes, contract addresses if anything is deployed on chain. Tick the sponsor bounties you apply to.
- **Judged on:** Creativity 10%, Feasibility 15%, Innovation and significance 25%, Code 50%. Details in `manual/judging.md`.
- **Prize:** one annual Urbe Hub membership for every member of the winning team, up to 3 people. One ranking, one winning team.
- **Bounties:** Arkiv $2,500, Swarm $1,000, Team1 $1,000, ENS $500, $5,000 in total, chosen and paid by the sponsors. All four briefs are in `manual/prizes.md`.
